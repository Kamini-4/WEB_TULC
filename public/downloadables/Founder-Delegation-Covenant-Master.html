1. Irrevocable Autonomous Signing Ceilings

The undersigned Founder and Executive Team hereby formally establish that direct reports possess 100% autonomous signature and procurement authority up to the thresholds listed below. Zero Founder notification, Slack ping, or verbal verification is required prior to execution.

Tier Classification Capital Ceiling Authorized Bench Mandatory Exception Rule
Tier 1: Tactical & Edge Under $5,000 Department Leads & Senior Managers Zero founder notification required. Logged in monthly ledger.
Tier 2: Operational Systems $5,000 to $25,000 VPs & General Managers Requires 2-party signoff. If ROI is positive within 90 days, auto-clears.
Tier 3: Strategic Reversible $25,000 to $100,000 COO / CFO / Executive Committee Must satisfy the Two-Door Reversibility Protocol (reversal cost < 15%).
Tier 4: Irreversible Core Above $100,000 Founder & Board of Directors Full board memorandum and risk contingency model required.

2. The Two-Way Door Escalation Filter

Before any decision or query is escalated to the Founder, the initiating executive must apply the following triage filter:

  • Is it a Type-2 (Reversible) Decision? If a poor outcome can be reversed within 14 days without fatal brand or financial erosion, the executive is instructed to execute immediately without consulting the Founder.
  • The "2 Solutions + Recommendation" Mandate: If an issue genuinely requires Founder alignment, the executive must arrive with: (a) The factual constraint, (b) Two viable operational options, and (c) Their unambiguous recommendation with expected trade-offs. Asking "What should we do?" is strictly forbidden.

Sovereign Governance Law

"Any manager who asks the Founder for permission on a decision within their authorized boundary is abdicating authority. The Founder will respond only with: 'You own the outcome. What does your boundary protocol dictate?'"

3. The Two-Week Step-Away Standard

This Covenant commits the organization to maintaining operational compounding during founder absences of up to 14 consecutive calendar days. Slack escalations marked "Urgent" during designated sabbatical windows are restricted exclusively to defined Category-A existential risks (e.g., active cybersecurity breaches, regulatory subpoenas, or catastrophic solvency incidents).
