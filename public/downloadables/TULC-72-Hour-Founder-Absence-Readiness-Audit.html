72-Hour Founder Absence Readiness Audit

CONFIDENTIAL DOCTRINE
CONTACT: suchita@theunboundedleadership.com

Score your organization across these 10 binary gates. If you were unreachable for 72 hours, which of these would trigger an emergency breakdown?

Part 1: Capital & Procurement Autonomy

Gate 01: Micro-Expenses (< $5,000): Can your team approve vendor renewals, contractor invoices, and ad spend without you opening Slack or signing a check?
Gate 02: Emergency Mitigation (< $25,000): Does your COO or Finance lead possess written mandate to deploy emergency contingency capital without verbal signoff?

Part 2: Client & Commercial Delivery

Gate 03: Escalation Firewall: If an angry key account demands a meeting with the CEO, does your executive team have a codified protocol to resolve it without escalating to you?
Gate 04: Sales Pipeline Motion: Can standard enterprise proposals and contracts close and execute without founder bespoke discounts or custom promises?

Part 3: Tactical Operational Rhythms

Gate 05: Meeting Autonomy: Do weekly executive syncs occur on time and produce binding decisions even if the founder is absent from the call?
Gate 06: Asynchronous Standups: Are project status updates maintained in writing without requiring the founder to chase deadlines verbally?

Part 4: People, Hiring & Culture

Gate 07: Hiring Signoff: Can department heads extend standard employment offers within authorized salary bands without founder final interviews?
Gate 08: Performance Accountability: If an employee underperforms, is there a codified escalation protocol that executes without founder hand-holding?

Part 5: Fiduciary & Key-Person Insulation

Gate 09: Password & Access Custody: Are all corporate domains, bank accounts, and critical credentials held in institutional password vaults with designated secondaries?
Gate 10: The 14-Day Sabbatical Test: Could you step completely offline for two weeks without revenue dropping or key staff resigning?

Diagnostic Grading Rubric:

8–10 Checks Passed: High Sovereign Institutional Health. The business is an investable, transferable asset.

5–7 Checks Passed: Moderate Founder Bottleneck. 10–15 hours wasted weekly on routine coordination friction.

< 5 Checks Passed: Severe Key-Person Fragility. Enterprise valuation is subject to heavy key-person discount.
